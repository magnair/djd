About

After 15 years in the shadows, Don Juan Dracula is back. Known for their electrifying live shows and genre-defying sound, the band became a cult phenomenon, setting festival stages ablaze across Norway, shaking up New York City, and leaving their mark in Beijing.

Blending explosive energy, anthemic hooks, and theatrical flair, Don Juan Dracula was never just a band—they were an experience. Now, with a fresh heartbeat on drums but the same core lineup, they return not to reminisce, but to reclaim their place.

“Burn”, their first new track since 2008, was released on February 14th. Self-produced, it marks the beginning of much more to come. The wait is over. The legends are reborn.

  • Henrik Lysell Vocals, guitar
  • Henning Krane Synth
  • Ola Eriksen Bass
  • Fredrik Fagerli Dahle Drums
  • Dino Trto Guitar
